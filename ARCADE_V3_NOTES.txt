EDUVERSE ARCADE V3 - GAMEPLAY POLISH

Updates:
- Organ Rescue: drag-and-drop organ cards to reorder them, with arrow-button controls retained for touch/accessibility. Incorrect feedback gives a directional hint without displaying the full correct order.
- Math Vault: incorrect code feedback now gives strategy/instruction only and does not reveal the answer. The keypad display shakes briefly after an incorrect attempt.
- Word Detective: incorrect report feedback prompts the learner to connect clues and qualify conclusions rather than giving away the conclusion.
- Culture Quest: incorrect feedback asks the learner to reconsider visitor needs and budget without revealing the exact combination.
- Code Runner: the robot now moves through the grid one step at a time so learners can observe and debug the program.
- Added interaction transitions and reduced-motion accessibility support.

To run:
1. Extract this ZIP to a new folder; keep your previous backup.
2. Open the extracted eduverse_backup_2.0 folder in VS Code.
3. Launch index.html with Live Server.
4. Open Arcade Edu and test each game.

This is still a prototype: content scope, accessibility, and curriculum alignment should be reviewed before classroom deployment.
